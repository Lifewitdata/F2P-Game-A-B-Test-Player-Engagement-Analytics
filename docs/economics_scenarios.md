# Thirty-Day Commercial Scenarios

The archived notebook reports approximate 30-day IAP ARPU of $0.173983 for control A and $0.161177 for treatment B. These are IAP-only descriptive values from a 400-transaction ledger, not full commercial LTV.

## Retention loss and revenue proxy

- D30 retention is 4.1637% in A and 3.0278% in B, a −1.1359 percentage-point treatment lift.
- On the 8,422-player B cohort, this gap equates to approximately **96 fewer D30 returners** versus the A retention rate.
- Valuing those 96 players at the control cohort's observed 30-day ARPU gives approximately **$16.70 of D30 retention-related revenue at risk**. This assumes the lost returners would generate average control-cohort ARPU; it is a proxy, not causal revenue attribution.
- Separately, the observed ARPU gap is about **−$0.0128 per assigned player**. On an equal-sized cohort of 8,422, B is approximately **$107.85 lower in observed IAP revenue** than A. This difference is not necessarily caused only by retention.

## Same-population rollout scenarios

The scenarios below scale variant ARPU to the same 16,924-player eligible population.

| Option | Projected 30-day IAP revenue | Versus all-A |
|---|---:|---:|
| Keep A | ~$2,944.49 | Baseline |
| Ship B | ~$2,727.76 | −$216.73 |
| Hybrid: 35% B / 65% A | ~$2,868.63 | −$75.86 |

The hybrid share is a planning assumption, not a randomized result. The analysis excludes ad revenue, platform fees, taxes, costs, and value after D30. It should not be presented as profit or extrapolated lifetime value.
