# Thirty-Day Commercial Scenarios

The supplied purchase ledger has 400 transactions and does not represent a complete daily revenue ledger for every player. The analysis therefore computes observed cumulative IAP revenue and ARPU per assigned player (including zero spenders), with explicit cohort denominators.

1. **Keep A:** retain the steady-reward control.
2. **Ship B:** expose all eligible users to the high-variance treatment.
3. **Hybrid:** 35% B mix as an illustrative planning scenario.

The hybrid share is an assumption, not an experimentally estimated effect. These are observed-window revenue scenarios, not profit or forecasted LTV. The data do not include ad revenue, platform fees, taxes, operating costs, or full lifetime observation.
